# Alumni Corporation Board Members
The ACB is made up of nine voting members (also referred to as directors): the undegraduate President and Treasurer of the Alpha Iota undergraduate chapter; the ACB President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer; and three specialized directors: Director of Chapter Housing, Director of Chapter Advising, and Director of Alumni Engagement.
